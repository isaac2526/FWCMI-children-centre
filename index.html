The heart behind the fundraiser

A community with a calling — and a growing need.

Finished Work of Christ Ministries International (FWCMI) runs a Children Centre in Wakiso District, Uganda, rooted in a simple conviction: every child deserves care, protection and the chance to grow spiritually, academically and socially.

As more children come through the centre, the facilities and resources have struggled to keep up. Teachers continue to serve with commitment, meals are prepared with limited resources, and some of the most basic infrastructure is still unfinished.

This fundraiser is about turning that reality into a new chapter — one where children can learn in better classrooms, eat more balanced meals, rest with dignity and play within a safer environment.

“Whoever welcomes one such child in my name welcomes me.”Matthew 18:5

We believe small acts of generosity, brought together, can create lasting change.

The year-one plan combines one-time infrastructure improvements with twelve months of essential recurring support. The total is a planning target from the September 2026 budget, not a live donation tally.

Complete sanitation facilities and secure the open compound with a perimeter fence and gate.

Better conditions for learning

Provide three additional classrooms, furniture, bedding and dependable teacher support.

Everyday care that lasts

Support balanced meals, kitchen essentials and consistent operating needs over the year.
